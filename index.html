Application

On in ten minutes

Step 1

Prep

Push back cuticles, buff the shine off your natural nail, and swipe with the alcohol pad. Every set comes with a prep kit.

Step 2

Size

Lay out all 10 nails and match one to each finger before you glue. Each should cover your nail wall to wall without touching skin.

Step 3

Press

Apply glue or an adhesive tab, press from cuticle to tip, and hold for 20 seconds. Avoid hot water for the first hour.

Sizing guide

Small or Medium width?

Every set comes with 10 nails, one for each finger, in a Small or Medium width. Measure across the widest part of your thumbnail to pick yours. Length (Short, Medium or Long) is up to you.
